Board: the `layout.Board` component (RFC 0005 slice 1). Columns with a label and count scroll sideways, each column scrolls on its own, cards open on click or Enter, a group-by picker switches the grouping, and a "Move to…" action moves a card by keyboard or touch. The `@yadad/core` peer range is now `^0.10.0` (the version that has the board key).
